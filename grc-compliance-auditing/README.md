# Enterprise GRC, Security Controls Management & International Audit Readiness

## 📋 Executive Summary
* **Domain:** Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC), Security Auditing, and Policy Enforcement.
* **Frameworks & Standards:** ISO/IEC 27001, PCI-DSS, HIPAA.
* **Core Objective:** Establish continuous compliance monitoring, automated evidence collection, and rigorous hardening assessments to maintain 100% audit readiness for internal evaluations and international regulatory bodies.

---

## 🔍 Key Responsibilities & Implementation (Formato STAR / Caso Técnico)

### 📌 1. Controls Mapping & Continuous Compliance Tracking
* **The Challenge:** Managing multi-standard frameworks (ISO 27001, PCI, HIPAA) required tracking hundreds of distinct security controls to ensure operational adherence across the enterprise.
* **Execution:** 
  * Implemented and managed a centralized compliance tracking system to monitor control effectiveness and calculate real-time compliance percentages.
  * Established recurring evidence collection cycles, auditing operational baselines such as mandatory quarterly vulnerability scans, endpoint protection/antivirus definition status, and patch management logs.
  * Identified compliance gaps proactively, flagging deficient or lagging controls before they impacted audit posture.

### 📌 2. Contractual & Regulatory Gap Analysis
* **The Challenge:** Evolving industry standards and updated business contracts frequently introduced new security requirements that needed to be mapped to existing internal controls.
* **Execution:** 
  * Conducted detailed legal and technical reviews of contract renewals, service level agreements (SLAs), and standard updates (e.g., ISO/IEC revisions or PCI-DSS version transitions).
  * Performed gap analyses to translate new legal or industry mandates into actionable technical controls and updated internal policies.
  * Integrated newly identified requirements into the GRC tracking framework to ensure seamless operational adoption.

### 📌 3. Hardening Audits & International Audit Readiness
* **The Challenge:** Preparing the organization to face stringent internal audits and international third-party certifications without finding critical non-conformities.
* **Execution:** 
  * Designed and executed comprehensive hardening and compliance audits across systems and infrastructure to evaluate real-time posture.
  * Simulated live audit conditions, reviewing technical configurations, access controls, and administrative logs against international compliance criteria.
  * Achieved and maintained a 100% readiness and compliance score during internal and international certification audits.
