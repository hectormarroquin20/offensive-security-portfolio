## 📁 Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) & Security Leadership

### 📌 1. Multi-Standard Compliance & International Audit Readiness (ISO 27001, PCI, HIPAA)
* **Context / Challenge:** Managing multi-framework compliance required tracking hundreds of technical and administrative controls to ensure continuous enterprise alignment with international standards.
* **Execution:** 
  * Implemented and maintained a centralized GRC system to monitor control effectiveness and calculate real-time compliance percentages.
  * Automated and executed recurring evidence collection cycles (e.g., quarterly vulnerability scans, antivirus definition currency, patch management logs) to identify and remediate compliance gaps proactively.
  * Designed and executed internal hardening audits, simulating live evaluations to ensure 100% audit readiness for internal and international third-party certification audits.

### 📌 2. Regulatory Gap Analysis & Contractual Security Integration
* **Context / Challenge:** Evolving industry standards, standard updates (e.g., ISO/IEC revisions, PCI-DSS updates), and renewed business contracts frequently introduced new security obligations.
* **Execution:** 
  * Conducted technical and legal reviews of contract renewals, service level agreements (SLAs), and regulatory framework updates.
  * Performed gap analyses to translate new legal mandates and contractual clauses into actionable technical controls and internal policies.

### 📌 3. Security Team Leadership, Communications & Corporate Awareness Programs
* **Context / Challenge:** Scaling security operations and maintaining a strong human firewall required active team coordination, clear company-wide communication channels, and enforced mandatory training compliance.
* **Execution:** 
  * Coordinated day-to-day operations and task allocation for security analysts, streamlining investigation and compliance workflows.
  * Managed the creation and dissemination of enterprise-wide security communications, incident alerts, and risk advisories.
  * Designed, coordinated, and tracked compliance for the company's annual mandatory security awareness training programs, measuring completion rates and ensuring high corporate adoption to mitigate human-factor risks.
